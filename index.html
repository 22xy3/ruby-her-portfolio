Who I Am

Strategist · Storyteller · Advocate

I'm Ruby Her, a Hmong-American and first-generation college student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison, studying Consumer Behavior & Marketplace Studies on the pre-business track with a focus in Marketing and Management.

My path into marketing began far from a classroom. As a teen health educator with Wisconsin PATCH and a participant in the Medical College of Wisconsin's StEP-UP program, I discovered the power of translating complex ideas into stories that move communities to act.

The best marketing doesn't just sell. It tells the truth beautifully, amplifies underrepresented voices, and builds bridges between cultures and communities.

I'm an All-in-Milwaukee Scholar, Milwaukee's Finest, and Kelben Foundation Scholar: reminders of the community that raised me and the future I'm building for those who come next.
